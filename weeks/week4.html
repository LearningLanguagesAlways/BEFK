Section C

부정문 & 질문

Negatives & Questions — Using "do" and "does"
현재 시제의 부정문과 질문에는 do/does를 써요. he/she/it에는 does, 나머지는 do를 써요.
Use do/does for negatives and questions. Use "does" with he/she/it, and "do" with everything else.

do / does 규칙 The do / does Rule

do
I · You · We · They
Do you work here?
여기서 일하세요?
does
He · She · It
Does she work here?
그녀는 여기서 일해요?
중요: does를 쓰면 동사는 기본형으로 돌아가요! "Does she works?" (X) → "Does she work?" (O)
Important: When you use "does," the main verb loses its -s and returns to the base form!
