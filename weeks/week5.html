Articles — A Big Challenge for Korean Speakers

Korean has no articles, so this is tricky. But a few simple rules will help you a lot!

관사 규칙 The Article Rules

핵심: a/an은 소리로 결정해요, 철자가 아니에요! "an hour" (h는 묵음), "a university" (you 소리)
Key: Choose a/an by the sound, not the spelling! "an hour" (silent h), "a university" (you-sound).

Plurals — One Hotel, Two Hotels
